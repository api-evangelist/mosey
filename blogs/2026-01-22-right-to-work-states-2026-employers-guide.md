Employment law can feel overwhelming with all its terms, rules, and exceptions. One term you may have come across is “right-to-work.” What exactly does it mean, and how does it affect both employers and employees in 2026? Right-to-work laws impact whether employees can be required to join a union or pay union dues as a condition of employment.
