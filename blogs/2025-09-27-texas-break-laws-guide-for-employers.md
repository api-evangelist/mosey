Expanding into Texas means understanding the state’s unique approach to workplace breaks: there aren’t any requirements for adult employees. While many other states mandate specific meal and rest periods, Texas gives employers complete discretion over break policies, creating both opportunities and compliance challenges. This freedom isn’t as simple as it appears.
