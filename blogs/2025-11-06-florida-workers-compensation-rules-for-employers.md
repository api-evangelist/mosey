Getting workers compensation wrong in Florida means immediate stop-work orders and penalties starting at $1,000. The Florida workers compensation rules for employers aren’t suggestions—they’re legal requirements enforced by the Florida Bureau of Compliance through job site inspections across the state. This guide to workers compensation covers who needs coverage, reporting obligations, and how to avoid penalties that catch thousands of businesses off guard.
