An equal opportunity employer (EEO) makes decisions about hiring, promotions, and other employment issues based solely on a person’s qualifications. They pledge not to discriminate based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, nationality, sexual orientation, gender identity, and other protected factors. Understanding EEO laws is essential for any business because it sets the standard for a fair, ethical, and inclusive workplace.
