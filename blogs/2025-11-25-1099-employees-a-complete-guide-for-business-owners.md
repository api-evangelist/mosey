Understanding the rules for 1099 employees versus W-2 workers matters if you run a business or manage a team. Often called independent contractors or freelancers, 1099 employees make up a significant chunk of today’s workforce. How you classify workers affects everything from tax obligations to benefits to legal responsibilities.
