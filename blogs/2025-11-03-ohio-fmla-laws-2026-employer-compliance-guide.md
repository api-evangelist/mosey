Understanding Ohio FMLA laws helps employers stay compliant while supporting employees who need time away from work. The Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) provides eligible employees with job protection if they need to take a leave of absence for qualifying reasons. Some states provide additional protections or mandate paid time off.
